---
name: brief
description: Start brief in five fields before a piece of work (goal, done when, stop points, not in scope, landing for today). Use when the user says "brief", "start brief" or begins a larger task or a building spree. Personal guard P6.
---
Ask for these five fields in ONE message, as a numbered list. Every field may be skipped with "-". Ask nothing else.

1. Goal: what should exist at the end?
2. Done when: how do we both see that it is finished?
3. Stop points: where do you want to look before I continue?
4. Not in scope: what do I leave alone?
5. Landing for today: a clock time or an artefact at which today's work ends.

Then repeat the brief back in at most five lines and start. Do not add questions.
During the work: at each stop point, stop and report. At the landing (field 5): write the state with the handoff command, say once "Landing reached: <time/artefact>", and offer nothing further. If the user continues, continue without comment.
For a free exploring spree only field 5 matters; say so if the user skips the rest.
